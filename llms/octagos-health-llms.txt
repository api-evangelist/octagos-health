# Octagos Health

  > AI-powered cardiac remote monitoring platform for cardiology practices and health systems. Octagos combines vendor-neutral device support, the Atlas AI engine for alert triage, EHR integrations (Epic, Oracle
   Health, athenahealth), clinical services, and patient-facing apps to reduce alert fatigue and accelerate cardiac monitoring workflows.

  Octagos serves cardiology practices, electrophysiology labs, and large health systems including AdventHealth, HCA Healthcare, University of Pennsylvania, INTEGRIS Health, Ochsner Health, UTMB Health, and many
  others. The platform supports all major cardiac device vendors (Medtronic, Boston Scientific, Abbott/St. Jude, Biotronik) and all session types: in-clinic device checks, remote transmissions, heart failure
  reports, loop recorder data, and CardioMEMS hemodynamic monitoring.

  ## Optional

  ### About the Atlas AI Engine

  Atlas AI is Octagos' proprietary triage engine that automatically classifies cardiac transmissions, suppresses false-positive alerts, and routes clinically meaningful events to the appropriate reviewer. It
  supports all major device manufacturers and is designed to reduce manual review burden on cardiology teams while improving response times to true clinical events.

  ### Supported Device Vendors

  - Medtronic (MDT)
  - Boston Scientific (BSX)
  - Abbott / St. Jude Medical (STJ)
  - Biotronik (BIO)
  - ARCS

  ### Supported Session and Device Types

  - In-clinic device interrogations and pacemaker checks
  - Remote transmissions from implantable cardiac devices (ICD, CRT, pacemakers, loop recorders)
  - Heart failure reports including CardioMEMS hemodynamic monitoring
  - Ambulatory ECG monitoring via the S-Patch EXL wearable patch
  - Loop recorder data
